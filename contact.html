Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries.

How quickly can you install a system?
For standard residential properties, we can typically survey and install within 48 hours of your initial contact. Commercial projects require a bespoke timeline based on scale.
Are your systems wireless?
We offer both hardwired and encrypted wireless solutions. Hardwired is recommended for enterprise, while wireless is often best for retrofitting residential homes.
Do you monitor the cameras?
No. Our 24/7 monitoring station only receives alarm triggers and metadata (like motion alerts). We do not have access to your live video feeds to ensure strict privacy.
